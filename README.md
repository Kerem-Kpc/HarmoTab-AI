# HarmoTab-AI 🎶

HarmoTab-AI is an **AI-driven music notation and tablature assistant**.  
It helps musicians, students, and educators **convert, analyze, and interact with sheet music** in a smarter way. By combining artificial intelligence with intuitive design, HarmoTab-AI makes learning and practicing music more engaging, accessible, and efficient.

---

## ✨ Features
- 🎼 **Smart Conversion**: Transform sheet music into interactive tablatures.
- 🤖 **AI Assistance**: Suggest chords, harmonies, and fingering patterns.
- 📚 **Educational Focus**: Designed for learners and teachers to simplify music theory.
- 🎹 **Multi-Instrument Support**: Guitar, piano, and more.
- 🌍 **Open Source**: Built to be extended and improved by the community.
